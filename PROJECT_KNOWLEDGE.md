Follow the same discipline the admin-dashboard project used: disclose uncertainty explicitly rather than reporting something as fixed before it's actually confirmed; note dead ends and retracted theories, not just what worked, so a future session doesn't retry them blind.
3. If a NEW FlutterFlow AI SDK/tooling gotcha is discovered (not specific to this project's own business logic — a DSL quirk, a codegen quirk, a CLI behavior), add it to `.cursor/rules/project_rules.md`'s "FlutterFlow quirks learned the hard way" section instead of (or in addition to) here, so it's visible in the pre-task ritual even for someone skimming only the rules file.
4. Keep numbering sequential (`§1`, `§2`, ...) and never renumber past entries — the admin-dashboard project's own numbering has gaps and out-of-order dates from revisions; that's fine, sequential-and-append-only is what matters, not perfectly tidy numbering.
